Task 2: Problem / cause - Solution essay 

_**The structure:**_
_Introduction:_
1. background statement ( the paraphrase of the question statement )
2. outline ( name one problem/cause and one solution)

_Body paragraph 1:_ 
1. topic sentence - introduce the problem/cause
2. explanation - come with details to support the topic sentence ( can write 2 or 3 sentences optional)
3. example - from your general knowledge or experience 

_Body parahgraph 2:_
1. topic - sentence - introduce the solution to the problem/cause above 
2. explanation - come with details to support your topic sentence ( 1, 2 or 3 sentences, complex or simple doesn't matter)
3. example - from your general knowledge or experience 

_Conclusion:_ 
1. The mirror of introduction / The summary of whole essay

Advanced Vocabulary

1. Introducing Problems
High-level phrases
a major issue
a serious concern
a growing problem
a pressing issue
a widespread challenge
a significant obstacle

Example
Traffic congestion has become a major issue in many large cities.

2. Describing Causes
Academic expressions
one major cause is…
a key factor is…
one contributing factor is…
this problem arises from…
this issue is largely caused by…
this can be attributed to…

Example
One major cause of traffic congestion is the increasing number of private vehicles.

3. Explaining Effects
Cause–effect language
as a result
consequently
therefore
this leads to…
this results in…
this gives rise to…

Example
As a result, commuters spend many hours stuck in traffic.

4. Introducing Solutions
Band 7–9 phrases
one possible solution is…
this issue could be addressed by…
this problem can be tackled by…
a practical approach would be to…
an effective measure would be to…
a long-term solution would be to…

Example
One possible solution is to improve public transportation systems.

5. Government Solutions
Useful phrases
governments should implement…
authorities could introduce…
policies should be developed to…
stricter regulations should be enforced
greater investment is required in…

Example
Authorities could introduce congestion charges in busy city centres.

6. Individual Solutions
Useful expressions
individuals should consider…
people can contribute by…
citizens should be encouraged to…
public awareness campaigns could promote…

Example
Citizens should be encouraged to use public transportation instead of private cars.


7. Evaluating Solutions
Academic language
this approach would significantly reduce…
this measure could help alleviate…
this policy may help address…
this strategy could be highly effective

Example
This measure could help alleviate traffic congestion in urban areas.

8. Concluding a Problem–Solution Essay
Band 7–9 conclusion phrases
In conclusion…
To conclude…
Overall…
Taking everything into consideration…

Example
In conclusion, although traffic congestion is a serious urban problem, effective policies and improved public transport could significantly reduce it.


High-Level Academic Words (Band 8–9)
Students can use these across many essays:
significant
substantial
considerable
dramatically
inevitably
consequently
crucial
fundamental
sustainable
beneficial
Example
It is crucial for governments to develop sustainable transportation systems.
Academic Verb Bank (Band 7–9)
Basic Verb	Academic Alternative
cause	lead to
solve	address
reduce	alleviate / minimise
increase	boost
improve	enhance
deal with	tackle
Example
Investing in public transport can alleviate traffic congestion.
Universal Problem–Solution Sentence
One effective way to address this issue would be for governments to invest more heavily in public transportation infrastructure.
