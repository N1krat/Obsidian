# Lesson 17 Reading

## Topic: Multiple Choice Questions 

Reading the instructions is very important!

_Tips and Strategy:_ 
1. Before looking at the options, only identify key words, also identify the focus (reason, opinion etc), this type of questions is full of trap words (only, all, always, mainly, primarily etc)
2. Locate the relevant paragraph, you need to follow the text order and focus on specific paragraphs, do not reread everything!
3. Scan for synonyms, paraphrasing, numbers and proper nouns 
4. We are not matching words, you are matching meaning!
5. Eliminate wrong options systematically
6. Be attentive if the answer sounds logical, if it makes sens in the real life but is not mentioned in the text then it is wrong

_Time strategy:_
1 min -> analyse the question 
1-2 min -> to find the paragraph 
30 sec -> try to eliminate destructors
