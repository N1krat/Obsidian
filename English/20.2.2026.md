# Lesson 16 Speaking

## Topic: Introduction to IELTS Speaking

The exam lasts for: _11-14 minutes_

The test consists of 3 parts: 
	Part 1 (4 - 5 min)
	Part 2 (3 - 4 min)
	Part 3 (4 - 5 min)

_Important details:_
- It is very important to fit in the time!!!
- The answers need to have a structure ( were we will include: your opinion, at least 2 supporting arguments, a short conclusion or final thought )
- Use as much paraphrasing as possible and as many unique words
- 4 criteria for IELTS speaking: pronunciation, lexical resource, grammatical range and accuracy, fluency and coherence
- _**Very important to speak slowly and clearly**_
