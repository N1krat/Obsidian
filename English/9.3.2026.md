_A well-balanced opinion essay must contain 4 paragraphs:_
1. _Introduction_ ( 40 - 50 words ), 3 sentences ( background statement - paraphrase of the question statemnt , thesis statement - your opinion, outline statement - 2 reasons to support opinion )
2. _Body paragraph 1_ ( 70 - 80 words ), 5 - 7 sentences ( Topic sentence, Explanation sentence/s, Example sentence/s ) 
3. _Body paragraph 2_ ( 70 - 80 words ), 5 - 7 sentence ( Topic sentence, Explanation sentence/s, Example sentence/s ) 
4. _Conclusion_ ( 40 - 50 words or 30 - 40 words ), 2 - 3 sentences ( mirror of the introduction )

_Examples:_
_**Introduction**_

_Background statement_ - In modern societies, career choices are often influenced by financial considerations as well as personal fullfilment.
_Thesis statement_ - While a high salary can provide financial security and the confortable life style, I believe job satisfaction is ultimately more important for long-term well-being. 
_Outline statement_ - This essay will disscus thy financial stability is attractive but argue that enjoying one's work leads to greater productivity and overall happiness. 

In modern societies, career decisions are often influenced by various factors including financial rewards and personal enjoyment, I strongly agree that having a high salary is more important than job satisfaction. This essay will explain, that a large income _provides financial security_ and also allows individuals to achieve a higher standart of living. 


_**Body paragraph 1:**_

The primary reason why a high salary is important is that it offers a financial security. People with well-paid jobs are able to cover essential expences such as housing, health-care, education and daily living costs without constant financial pressure/worry. Financial stability also reduces stress an enables individuals to plan for the future, including saving money and investing. For instance professionals working in sectors such as medicine or engineering often earn high salaries that allow them to support their families and maintain financial independence. 


_**Body paragrah 2:**_

Another significant advantage of earning a high income is the _improved quality of life_ it provides.  A large salary enables individuals to enjoy better living conditions , travel, pursue hobbies and access high-quality services. Even if a job is not particularly enjoyeble the financial benefits can compensate by allowing the people to enjoy their free time and personal life, for example, in my home country many employees choose demanding corporate jobs because the high income allows them to travel frequently and afford confortable life styles. 

_**Conclusion:**_

In conclusion, although job satisfaction has some value, financial rewards are by far more significant. A high salary not only ensures financial security but also provides a better quality of life which is why it should be considered the most important factor when choosing a career.

_**Opinion essay 2:**_
_Introduction:_

It is often argued, that today teleworking is a far better experience than working in an office. While working in an office grants a lot of control for the management of the company, in my opinion I partially agree with the statement because a remote job is far more flexible than that in the office. This essay will explain, that teleworking is more flexible and more enjoyable but has less control over the employees in comparison with work in the office.

 The primary reason why teleworking is so popular is its flexibility. People with remote jobs have more opportunities to have a more flexible schedule and to use the free time that they have for other activities such as family time, hobbies or self-developing activities. For instance, when working remotely we can manage more thoroughly our schedule and time manage everything more individually for us, this means doing tasks in a comfortable rhythm.

A disadvantage of a remote job is the freedom that the employee has. In modern office jobs superiors always want to monitor their employees, because they always want to see results in the shortest time possible. This creates a uncomfortable environment in which the employees can’t work at full capacity, but at the same time we always need to monitor the employees tasks, because nobody needs an employee that’s not working properly.

In conclusion, although remote jobs give more opportunities for the employee such as time management or a more comfortable environment, a strict monitoring process is still required. All types of employees need to have an strict work regime for a more strict and transparent work process which in the end needs to grant high efficiency and job completion.
