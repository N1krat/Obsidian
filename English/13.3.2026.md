_Overview:_
1. Overall, it is clear that ( , is obligatory )
2. It is evident that 
3. The most notable feature is
4. A general upward trend can be observed ( tendinta de crestere generala )
5. A significant decline is apparent 
6. The figures fluctuate considerably 
7. The data show a steady increase / decrease

_Decreasing / Increasing_
verbs:

| Increasing | Decreasing                 |
| ---------- | -------------------------- |
| rise       | decline                    |
| climb      | drop                       |
| grow       | fall                       |
| surge      | plummet ( o cadere brusca) |
| soar       | diminish                   |
| escalate   |                            |

nouns:

| Increase          | Decrease   |
| ----------------- | ---------- |
| a rise            | a decrease |
| an increase       | a decline  |
| a surge           | a fall     |
| a dramatic growth |            |

adverbs:

sharply, steadly, considerably, gradualy

ex: 
The number of tourists rose sharply in 2015
The number of tourists increased dramaticaly

_Describing stability:_

remain stable, remain constant, stay relatively unchanged, to level of, to plateau 

ex: 
The percantage _remained relatively stable_ during the following decade

The percantage stayed relatively unchanged during the following decade
The percantage remained stable during the following decade 
The percantage remained constant during the following decade
The percantage leveled during the following decade 
The percantage plateuded during th following decade 

--- 

_Describing fluctuations:_

to fluctuate, to vary considerably, experience ups and downs, show irregular changes

ex: 
The figures fluctuated slightly throughout the period 

The figures varied considerably throughout the period
The figures experienced ups and downs throughout the period 
The figures showed irregular changes throughout the period 

--- 

_Comparing  Data:_

In comparison with, in contrast, similarly, whereas, while, whilst, compare to

ex: 
While France experienced a steady increase, Germany saw a slight decline. 

Compared France experienced a steady increase, Germany saw a slight decline

_Quantifying Data:_

around, roughly, approximately, nearly, just over, just lower 

ex: 
The figure stood at approximately 40%

The figure stood at around 40%
The figure stood at roughly 40%
The figure stood at just over 40%
The figure stood at just lower 40%
