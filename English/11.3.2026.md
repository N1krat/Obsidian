# Lesson 25 Writing 

## Topic: Writing Opinion Essays

_**Useful expressions for the opinion essay:**_

_Background statement:_
1. It is often argued that
2. Many people believe that
3. There is a common view that
4. It is widely accepted that 
5. A growing number of people believe that 
6. Some people claim that

Many people believe that, a high salary is more important than job satisfaction

---

_Giving your opinion expressions:_
1. In my opinion
2. From my perspective
3. I strongly believe that
4. I am convinced that 
5. I firmly support the idea that ... ( good one )
6. I tend to agree that 
7. I completely disagree with the idea that ( disagreing one )

In my opinion, job satisfaction plays a more important role in long therm career success 

--- 
_Strong opinion language:_

1. I firmly believe that 
2. I am strongly convinced that
3. I wholeheartedly agree that
4. I strongly oppose the view that 
5. I fully support the argument that

I firmly believe, that financial stability alone can not guarantee professional fulfilment

_wholeheartedly_ - to do something with complete sincerity, enthusiasm, and without any doubt or reservation

---

_Expressions to introduce arguments:_

1. One important reason is that
2. One major argument in favour of this is that
3. The primary reason for this is 
4. Another key point to consider is 
5. An additional argument is that
6. A further explanation is that 

One impornant reason is that job satisfaction contributes significantly to mental wellbeing

---

_Giving examples:_

1. For example 
2. For instance 
3. A good ilustration of this is 
4. This can be clearly be seen in 
5. A clear example of this is 
6. This is particularly evident in

A clear example of this can be seen in the creative industries. 

---

_Comparing ideas:_

1. On the one hand 
2. On the other hand 
3. By comparison 
4. In contrast
5. However 
6. Nevertheless

On one hand, a high sallary provides financial security, on the other hand job satisfaction contribuites to long therm happines
